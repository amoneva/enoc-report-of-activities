<#meeting-minutes>

*Date*: 2026-01-15; 13:00 *Present*: Alex, Asier *Absent*: Isabelle /
Note/: The person responsible for the task is listed next to the section
heading.

 1. Open Science Awards (Asier)

  * NSCR has agreed to sponsor the ENOC Early Career Open Science Award
    with €1500 annually for an initial period of 3 years, after which
    continuation will be evaluated.
  * No specific conditions will be linked to the award (e.g.,
    collaboration with or visiting NSCR).
  * The award may allow recipients to participate in ESC meetings by
    covering travel and accommodation.
  * It is unclear whether formal approval from the ESC Board is required
    to establish the award.
  * The ESC Board has not replied to our emails yet. The last reminder
    was forwarded to the Executive Secretariat.

Action point: Alex to send another reminder to the ESC Board.

 2. Website (Alex)

  * Blog entries are in a strange order.

Action point: Alex to attempt a fix.

 3. Summer School (Isabelle)

  * Sandy Schumann has confirmed giving a talk at the Summer School.
    Further details to be confirmed.

Action point: Isabelle to follow-up on this.

 4. Newsletter (Isabelle)

  * Planned content includes an introduction of the Co-Chairs and
    proposed goals, as well as an overview of initiatives carried out so
    far.

Action point: Isabelle to follow-up on this.

 5. Ideas to be taken to EUROCRIM for discussion (Alex)

  * Creating an Open Science Curriculum
  * Organizing an Open Science Summer School. This idea is conditional
    on obtaining a Cologne Summer Schools grant (https://www.uni-
    koeln.de/sites/international/aaa/93/93file/
    CSS_Information_for_Faculty_Members/2026_CSS_Call_for_Funding_Cologne_Summer_Schools.pdf <https://www.uni-koeln.de/sites/international/aaa/93/93file/CSS_Information_for_Faculty_Members/2026_CSS_Call_for_Funding_Cologne_Summer_Schools.pdf>).

 6. Open Science NL funding (Asier)

  * The Open Science NL Work Programme for 2026-2017 has been published
    (https://www.openscience.nl/nieuws/werkprogramma-2026-2027-
    nederland-zet-volgende-stap-naar-open-science <https://
    www.openscience.nl/nieuws/werkprogramma-2026-2027-nederland-zet-
    volgende-stap-naar-open-science>)
  * The call for "Open Science Meetings" could be of interest to ENOC
  * Potential co-organization with NSCR for a small-scale meetings
  * Could be a follow-up to the Cologne meeting, perhaps with a
    different structure and/or on a different topic
  * Funding instrument operates on a first come, first served basis.
  * Applicants must be affiliated with eligible knowledge institutions

Action point: Asier to check the eligibility criteria and the
publication date of the funding call

 7. Contribution to the ESC Newsletter (Asier)

  * Still no response from the Newsletter Editor.
  * An alternative idea to publication in the ESC newsletter would be
    submitting the contribution as a commentary to the new journal
    Evidence-Based.

Action point: Asier to check with Torbjörn whether a reminder has been sent.

 8. Any other business (Asier)

  * Scheduling of the next meeting to be discussed. Tentatively set to
    26 February from 11:00 to 12:00 hours.
  * Update the activity report, including:
      o Invitation of Sandy Schumann to the Summer School
      o Secured funding for the Open Science Award
