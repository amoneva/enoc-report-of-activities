## 1. Awards
- ESC Board sponsorship decision. Marcelo has not yet communicated the ESC Board's decision regarding sponsorship of the ENOC awards.
> Action point: Asier will follow up with Marcelo.

- Eligibility criteria for the two proposed awards. ENOC defines an Early Career Researcher (ECR) as a scholar who is within four years post-PhD. This definition aligns with eligibility criteria typically used in Dutch and German talent grant schemes.

- Nominations. Self-nominations are not accepted for the Open Criminology Award. ENOC reserves the right to declare an award vacant if no suitable candidate is identified.

- Supporting materials. Applicants must provide examples of contributions demonstrating alignment with the award criteria:
	+ ECR applicants: maximum of 3 contributions
	+ Senior applicants: maximum of 5 contributions
This limit is intended to facilitate a qualitative assessment.

- Application procedure. To simplify the application process, ENOC will develop a brief online questionnaire through which applicants can:
	+ Provide contact details of the nominee
	+ Upload the required supporting contributions as separate files
> Action point: Alex will send a concept of the questionnaire around.
> Action point: ENOC Co-Chairs will revise the eligibility and award criteria and circulate updates by email within the next week.

- Timeline. Applications for the Open Criminology Award will open once the awards are approved by the ESC Board. The application deadline will be set with sufficient lead time to allow for customization of the award (timeline to be determined).

- Award format and ceremony. The award will be presented as a paper certificate rather than a physical trophy. While awardees are not required to be present at the ceremony, attendance is strongly encouraged.

- Selection committee. The selection committee will consist of three members. If sponsorship is approved by the ESC Board, the committee will include two ENOC Co-Chairs and one representative of the sponsor(s). Any conflicts of interest will be carefully considered and managed.
